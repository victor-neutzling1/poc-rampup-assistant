# Team Rituals and Communication

> Fictional company and content, created for the Ramp-Up Assistant PoC.

Owner: Marcus Webb (Engineering Manager, Stock and Counter Web) · Also ask: Camille Martin (Engineering Onboarding Lead)

## Sprints

Squads work in two-week sprints that start on Mondays.

- **Planning** – Monday 10:00 local time, 1 hour.
- **Stand-up** – every day at 09:30 local time, 15 minutes, in the squad's Slack huddle.
- **Review** – last Friday of the sprint, squads demo to their product manager.
- **Retro** – right after the review, 45 minutes.

## Jira workflow

Every piece of work has a Jira ticket in the squad's project: `STK` (Stock), `ORD` (Orders), `WEB` (Counter Web), `MOB` (Kitchen Mobile), `PLAT` (Platform). IT requests go to the `IT` project.

Ticket statuses: `To Do` → `In Progress` → `In Review` → `Done`. Move a ticket to `In Review` when the pull request is open, and to `Done` only after the change is deployed to production.

## Slack etiquette

- Ask questions in public channels instead of direct messages, so the answer helps the next person too.
- Use threads. Post one message per topic and keep the discussion in its thread.
- `#eng-help` is for any engineering question. Nobody will judge a "basic" question.
- `@here` is only for things that need attention within the hour.

## Meetings

Wednesday afternoons are meeting-free for engineering. Do not book meetings with engineers between 13:00 and 17:00 local time on Wednesdays.
